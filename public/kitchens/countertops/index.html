Subway tile, mosaics, natural stone, or large-format panels: your backsplash protects your walls and sets the tone for the whole room. We coordinate it with your countertops, or add it on its own as one of the fastest ways to transform your kitchen.

Prefer less cleaning? Our grout-free backsplash panels give you the look of tile with a smooth surface that wipes clean in seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What countertop materials do you offer?

Quartz, granite, and solid surface, fabricated and installed by our team.

How do you make sure the countertops fit?

We digitally template your existing layout for a tight, accurate fit before fabrication.

What backsplash materials do you offer?

Ceramic, porcelain, and glass tile, natural stone and mosaics, and grout-free panels.

Can a backsplash be installed without replacing my countertops?

Yes. A new backsplash can be added on its own, and it is one of the fastest ways to transform your kitchen. We coordinate it with your existing countertops and cabinets.

Can you add a pot filler, filtered water, or a disposal?

Yes. We can add filtered water, a pot filler, a garbage disposal, or a soap dispenser.
