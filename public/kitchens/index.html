One Team, Start to Finish

Juggling separate cabinet, countertop, and plumbing contractors is stressful. With Bulldog Kitchen & Bath, one design consultant and one installation crew handle your entire kitchen, on one schedule and one warranty. Since 1993, Bulldog Kitchen & Bath has been designing and installing kitchens that fit the way families cook and gather.

It starts with an in-home design consultation. From there we handle cabinet replacement or refacing, countertops fabricated to fit, and the backsplash, sinks, faucets, and lighting.

What Our Cabinets Are Made Of

Quality you can see, built from materials that last.

Solid Wood

We offer solid wood cabinet doors in a variety of hardwoods, from birch to oak.

Plywood

Our cabinet boxes are built from plywood for long-lasting strength and durability.

MDF / HDF

Engineered from compressed wood fibers, it resists water, warping, and cracking.

NoParticle Board

It soaks up water, cracks easily, and wears out sooner, so we don't use it.
