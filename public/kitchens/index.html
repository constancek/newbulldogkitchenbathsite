Frequently Asked Questions

What kitchen services do you offer?

We install new, refaced, or refinished cabinets, countertops, backsplashes, sinks and faucets, and cabinet organizers, all designed and installed by one team.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Many kitchen upgrades are finished in as little as three days. A full kitchen with new cabinets takes longer, and your project coordinator gives you a detailed schedule before work starts.

Should I replace or reface my cabinets?

If your cabinet boxes are solid, refacing gives you a brand-new look in a fraction of the time and cost of a tear-out. If they are worn or damaged, we will recommend full replacement.

Can I update just one part of my kitchen?

Yes. You can start with new countertops, a backsplash, a sink and faucet, or organizers, or plan a full remodel. Your designer helps you choose what makes the biggest difference.

Do your promotions apply to kitchens?

Yes. Our current offer of $1,250 off applies to a full kitchen or bathroom remodel, and financing is available on approved credit. See Current Promos for details.
