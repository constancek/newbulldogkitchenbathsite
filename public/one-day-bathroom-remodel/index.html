Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really remodel a bathroom in one day?

Many tub, shower, and wall-system replacements are finished in as little as one day. Every product is custom-measured and prepared before install day, so our crew can remove the old and install the new in a single visit.

Which projects can be done in one day?

Tub and shower replacements, tub-to-shower conversions, and new wall systems are often finished in one day. Layout changes, plumbing moves, or full gut remodels take longer, and we tell you up front.

What happens before install day?

We measure your bathroom at the consultation and help you choose products and finishes. Then your base, walls, and trim are made to your exact measurements before we arrive.

Do I need to be home during the installation?

We ask that an adult be home at the start and end of install day so we can review the work with you.

Do you clean up afterwards?

Yes. We clean up, show you how to care for your new bath, and register your warranty before we leave.
