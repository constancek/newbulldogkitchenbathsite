Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get one of these looks in my home?

Yes. Tell your design consultant which look you like, and we will recreate it with our materials, sized to your space.

Can I mix and match colors and finishes?

Yes. Wall colors, patterns, fixtures, and hardware finishes can all be combined to make the look your own.

Can I see the design before I commit?

Yes. We can show you your new kitchen or bath in 3D before anything is ordered.

Do you offer looks for kitchens as well as bathrooms?

Yes. You will find both kitchen and bathroom styles here, and we design and install both.

How do I get started?

Request an estimate and mention the look you like. A design consultant will measure your space and bring samples.
