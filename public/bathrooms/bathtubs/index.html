Family-Friendly Tub & Shower Combos

A tub-and-shower combo is still the most practical choice for many homes. We pair a comfortable tub with a full wall system, grab bars, and storage so the whole family can use it every day, all at an affordable price.

  • Easy Maintenance: Non-porous surfaces resist mold and mildew, so keeping your new tub clean takes a quick wipe instead of a scrub brush.
  • Customizable Style: Choose from stone looks, tile patterns, solid colors, trim, and fixtures to match any bathroom.

Frequently Asked Questions

What bathtub styles do you offer?

Classic alcove tubs, standard-depth tubs, and deep soakers, plus tub-and-shower combos for everyday family use.

Can you turn my shower back into a tub?

Yes. We can convert an existing shower back to a tub and finish it with a matching wall surround and trim.

Does a new tub come with new walls?

You can pair your tub with a seamless wall surround in the color and pattern you choose for a finished, spa-like look.

Will my new tub chip or stain?

Our bathtubs are built to resist chips, stains, and fading, and they clean up with mild soap and water.

How long does a tub replacement take?

A new tub and wall surround is often installed in a single day. We confirm your timeline at the consultation.
