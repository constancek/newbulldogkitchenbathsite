Safer Bathrooms, Beautiful Design

We design accessible bathrooms that look like any other upscale remodel. Designed for Aging in Place

Planning ahead? Our consultants walk through your daily routine and recommend the right mix of safety features so your bathroom keeps working for you for years to come.

  • Walk-In Tubs: Step in through a sealed door instead of climbing over a high tub wall.
  • Roll-In Showers: Barrier-free bases with no curb, so a wheelchair or walker can roll straight in.
  • Low-Threshold Bases: A low step makes everyday showering safer without a full roll-in conversion.
  • Tub-to-Shower Conversions: Replace a hard-to-use tub with an easy-access shower in as little as one day.
  • Seats, Grab Bars & Handhelds: Fold-down seats, decorative grab bars, and handheld showers wherever you need them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will an accessible bathroom look clinical?

No. We design accessible bathrooms that look like any upscale remodel, and every grab bar comes in a decorative finish that matches your fixtures.

Which safety features can you add?

Barrier-free and low-threshold shower bases, fold-down seats, grab bars, handheld showers, easy-reach controls, and walk-in tubs.

Do you install roll-in showers for wheelchairs?

Yes. Our barrier-free bases have no curb, so a wheelchair or walker can roll straight in.

Can you add grab bars to my existing shower?

Yes. We can add grab bars, fold-down seats, and handheld showers as part of a remodel or as safety upgrades.

How do I know which features I need?
