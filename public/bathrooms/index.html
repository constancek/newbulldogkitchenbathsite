Frequently Asked Questions

What bath services do you offer?

Showers, bathtubs, tub-to-shower conversions, walk-in tubs, accessibility features, shower enclosures, vanities, and accessories, with wall systems in a wide range of colors and patterns.

Can I combine several upgrades in one project?

Yes. Many homeowners pair a new tub or shower with a wall system, a glass door, a vanity, and accessories. Your design consultant plans everything together so it all matches.

Which bath option is right for me?

It depends on how you use your bathroom. A tub-to-shower conversion suits people who rarely take baths, a walk-in tub or accessibility upgrades help anyone with limited mobility, and a new tub with a matching wall surround works well for families. Your design consultant helps you decide.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Many tub, shower, and wall-system replacements are done in as little as one day. Larger projects take longer, and we give you a timeline before work starts.

Is your bath work covered by a warranty?

Yes. Everything we install, including shower bases, bathtubs, and wall systems, carry a lifetime limited warranty for as long as you own your home, and our installation is guaranteed.
