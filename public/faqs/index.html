At Bulldog Kitchen & Bath, we work hard to make remodeling simple. Here are answers to common questions. To learn more, give us a call and speak with a knowledgeable design consultant.

Cost & Financing

How much does a bathroom or kitchen remodel cost?

Every project is different. Price depends on the size of your space, the products you choose, and any plumbing or structural work needed. We give you a clear, written quote after your consultation.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We offer several financing options on approved credit, including promotional no-interest plans and terms up to 60 months, to help make your remodel more affordable with manageable monthly payments. Visit our Financing page for details.

What happens at the consultation?

We measure your space, walk through product and finish options with you, and provide a clear, written estimate for your project.

Installation Process

Can you really remodel a bathroom in one day?

Many tub, shower, and wall-system replacements are completed in as little as one day. Projects involving layout changes, plumbing moves, or full gut remodels take longer, and we'll give you a timeline up front.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

A countertop or backsplash update can take a few days; a full kitchen with new cabinets typically takes a few weeks. Your project coordinator will provide a detailed schedule.

Do I need to be home during installation?

We ask that an adult be home at the start and end of each install day so we can review the work with you.

Products & Materials

What are your shower and tub walls made of?

Our wall systems are made from durable, non-porous materials that resist mold, mildew, and stains, with no grout lines to clean.

Can I see samples before I decide?

Yes. Your design consultant brings samples to your home, and we can create a 3D design of your new space.

How do I clean my new surfaces?

Most surfaces just need mild soap and water. Avoid abrasive pads and harsh chemicals. We'll leave care instructions after your install.

Kitchen Remodeling

Do you replace cabinets or reface them?

Both. If your cabinet boxes are in good shape, refacing gives you a new look for less time and money. If not, we'll recommend full replacement.

What countertop materials do you offer?

We install quartz, granite, and solid-surface countertops, among others. Your designer will help you compare durability, maintenance, and price.

Can you move plumbing or appliances?

In many cases, yes. Layout changes are reviewed during your consultation so they can be included in your quote and schedule.

Tub-to-Shower & Shower-to-Tub Conversions

Can you turn my bathtub into a walk-in shower?

Yes, tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most popular projects and are often completed in a single day.

Will removing my tub hurt resale value?

It depends on your home. We generally recommend keeping at least one tub in homes with young children; your consultant can help you decide.

Walk-In Tubs & Accessibility

Who is a walk-in tub right for?

Walk-in tubs are ideal for anyone who has trouble stepping over a standard tub wall, including seniors and people with limited mobility.

Can you add grab bars and seats to my existing shower?

Yes. We can add grab bars, fold-down seats, and handheld showers as part of a remodel or as standalone safety upgrades.

Warranty & Support

What warranty do you offer?

Our bath products are backed by a lifetime limited warranty, and our installation workmanship is guaranteed. See our Warranty page for details.

Who do I call if I have a problem?

Call or email us directly, and you'll talk to our own team, not a call center.

How soon can you start?
