Frequently Asked Questions

What does Bulldog Kitchen & Bath do?

We design and install kitchen and bathroom remodels: showers, bathtubs, wall systems, vanities, cabinets, countertops, backsplashes, and sinks and faucets. Our own crews handle the installation.

Do you offer any discounts?

Yes. We currently offer $1,250 off a full kitchen or bathroom remodel, plus special savings for homeowners 65 and up and for active-duty military and veterans. See Current Promos for details.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Cincinnati, Ohio and the surrounding Ohio, KY & IN tri-state area. Call us at (513) 657-3750 to confirm service at your address.

How do I get a price for my project?

Request an estimate. A design consultant measures your space, walks you through material and finish options, and gives you a clear, written quote.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. On approved credit, we offer terms up to 60 months and promotional plans with no interest for 24 months. Visit our Financing page for details.
