Swapping a rarely used bathtub for a spacious walk-in shower is one of the most popular bathroom upgrades, and for good reason. It makes the room feel bigger, safer, and easier to clean.

## Benefits of converting

- **Easier access:** a low-threshold base removes the need to climb over a tub wall.
- **More space:** a glass door and open layout make small bathrooms feel larger.
- **Less cleaning:** seamless wall panels mean no grout lines to scrub.

## When to keep a tub

If you have young children or it's the only tub in the house, it may make sense to keep one. Your design consultant can help you weigh daily use against resale.

## How long does it take?

Most conversions are completed in as little as one day once your materials are ready.
