Cabinets set the look of your kitchen, and they're usually the largest part of the budget. Here's how to decide between refacing and replacing.

## Choose refacing if…

- Your cabinet boxes are solid and free of water damage.
- You like your current layout.
- You want a faster project with less disruption.

## Choose replacement if…

- You want to change the layout or add an island.
- Boxes are damaged, sagging, or poorly built.
- You want specialized storage throughout.

Not sure? A design consultation will tell you which option makes sense for your kitchen.
